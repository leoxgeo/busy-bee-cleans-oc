🤝 Giving Back to Our Community

Donate to Support Families in Need

At Busy Bee Baby Gear Spa, we believe every child deserves a safe and healthy start. That's why we're dedicated to cleaning, sanitizing, and delivering donated baby items to local Orange County charities, ensuring they reach families who need them most.

🎁 Donate Baby Items

Have gently used baby gear you're no longer using? Donate it to us! We’ll clean and sanitize your items, making them good as new before delivering them to local charities.

💛 Monetary Support

Financial contributions help cover medical-grade sanitization materials and purchase essential safety items like car seats for families in need.

🏢 Partner Organizations

We proudly partner with and distribute gear through:
• MOMS Orange County
• Pueblos of California
• Starts With One Today
